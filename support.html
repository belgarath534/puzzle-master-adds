Puzzle Master Support

Need help with Puzzle Master, found a bug, or want your account data deleted? We're happy to help.

Contact

Open a request on our support page on GitHub. Tell us what happened, your device, and the category or mode you were playing.

Common questions

How do I save my progress? Progress is saved on your device automatically. Sign in with Google from the game to back it up and use it on another device.

I bought coins but didn't get them. Close and reopen the game. If they still aren't there, contact us with the date of the purchase.

Can I play with VoiceOver? Yes. Puzzle Master is built to work with VoiceOver, and questions and answers are read aloud.

How do I delete my account data? Contact us and we will delete your account, cloud save and leaderboard entries.
